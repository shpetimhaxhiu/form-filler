Form Autofill Bookmarklet

Automatically fill forms with realistic data using AI

How to Use

  1. Drag one of the following bookmarklets to your bookmarks bar:
    Form Autofill (Full) Form Autofill (Minified)
    The minified version works exactly the same but has a smaller file size.
  2. Visit any website with forms
  3. Click the bookmarklet in your bookmarks bar
  4. Click on the form you want to autofill
  5. Click again to confirm your selection
  6. Enter your OpenAI API key when prompted
  7. Wait for the AI to generate and fill in the form with realistic sample data
